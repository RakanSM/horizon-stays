# Gold & Brown Palette Preview — 2026-08-25

The public Horizon interface was visually reviewed locally in English in both light and dark modes after restoring the brand palette. The light scene now uses warm cream, espresso, and gold; the dark scene uses espresso brown, gold labels, and high-contrast ivory text.

The restored palette is token-driven across the public map, search, scroll scene, trust dialog, and primary actions. Public source regression tests confirm the previous public violet tokens are absent.
